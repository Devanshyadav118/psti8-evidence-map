## C. KNOWLEDGE SUMMARY

### Established
* **Peptide identity and target engagement:** PSTi8 (`PEGKGEQEHSQQKEEEEEMAV-NH2`, CAS 2434805-63-5, MW 2427.51 Da) is a 21-amino-acid C-terminally amidated, C-terminally truncated variant of pancreastatin (also referred to as PSTC$\Delta$8) developed at CSIR-CDRI that competitively antagonizes pancreastatin at the GRP78 binding site and inhibits GRP78 ATPase activity (`valicherla2018`, `valicherla2022_pk`, `garg2023_review`).
* **Pharmacokinetics and bioavailability:** Validated SPE-LC-ESI-MS/MS assays in rodent plasma demonstrate high water solubility (9.30 mg/mL in simulated gastric fluid; 25.75 mg/mL in intestinal fluid), >69% plasma protein binding, high stability in plasma and liver microsomes, ~95.0% intraperitoneal bioavailability, and ~78.5% subcutaneous bioavailability in healthy male and female Sprague-Dawley rats, with non-linear dose proportionality and no significant sex difference in PK parameters (`valicherla2020_jpba`, `valicherla2022_pk`).
* **Hepatic glucose and lipid regulation across models:** PSTi8 suppresses hepatic gluconeogenesis (downregulating PEPCK and G6Pase), enhances glycogenesis/glycolysis, reduces hepatic lipid accumulation, and improves glucose/insulin/pyruvate tolerance across male HFD, HFrD, db/db, STZ-induced, and dexamethasone-induced fatty liver/T2D mouse models as well as in HepG2 hepatocytes (`valicherla2018`, `gupta2019_dex`, `reza2021_mito`, `reza2022_lfs`, `garg2023_ejp`).
* **Hepatic signaling pathways:** PSTi8 restores hepatic insulin sensitivity via IRS1/2–PI3K–AKT–GSK3$\beta$/FoxO1 and PKC$\lambda/\zeta$–SREBP1c signaling, activates AMPK via GRP78 (increasing ACC and SREBP-1c phosphorylation in dexamethasone-treated mice), downregulates palmitate-induced NOX3/JNK oxidative stress, protects hepatic mitochondrial function (OCR, MMP, ATP), and—in combination with metformin—inhibits Fetuin-A and ER stress (`valicherla2018`, `gupta2019_dex`, `singh2019_menopause`, `singh2020_fetuina`, `singh2020_heliyon`, `reza2021_mito`, `reza2022_lfs`).
* **Adipose tissue and skeletal muscle immunometabolism:** In 3T3-L1 adipocytes, L6 myotubes, J774A.1 macrophages, and DIO/HFD+STZ mice, PSTi8 enhances GLUT4 translocation and glucose disposal, suppresses palmitate-induced lipolysis and ROS, improves mitochondrial membrane potential and oxygen consumption rate, and shifts immune populations from pro-inflammatory M1 macrophages and CD4$^+$/CD8$^+$ T cells toward anti-inflammatory M2 macrophages, Tregs, and eosinophils (`gupta2019_obesity`, `gupta2020_skeletal`, `goand2023_peptides`, `goand2023_lifesci`, `garg2023_aminoacids`).
* **NASH/MASH and female reproductive-metabolic models:** PSTi8 attenuates hepatic steatohepatitis, fibrosis ($\alpha$-SMA), and M1 macrophage inflammation while promoting iTregs in male MCD-diet NASH mice (`dadge2023_nash`, `lu2025_fimmu`), and restores insulin sensitivity, estrous cyclicity, ovarian morphology, and steroidogenic balance comparably to metformin in female menopausal (VCD+OVX+HFD, VCD+HFrD) and PCOS (letrozole+HFD, DHEA+HFD) rat models (`singh2019_menopause`, `singh2020_heliyon`, `yadav2024_pcos_let`, `yadav2025_pcos_dhea`).

### Contested
* **Sex-independence vs. sex-dependent disease outcomes:** While healthy rat pharmacokinetic studies reported no sex differences in systemic PSTi8 exposure (`valicherla2022_pk`), the only head-to-head both-sex disease model study (intrarectal PSTi8 in acute DSS colitis and homeostatic mice) found sex-dependent colonic epithelial cell responses, where PSTi8 delayed colitis onset in males but transiently elevated daily disease activity index at 72–96 hours in females without significant differences in cumulative or macroscopic scores (`tshikudi2024_colitis`, `tshikudi2024_jcag`).
* **Primary upstream receptor mechanism:** Across preclinical studies, PSTi8 is modeled both as a competitive antagonist occupying the endoplasmic reticulum chaperone GRP78 binding site (`valicherla2018`, `gupta2019_dex`, `garg2023_review`) and in schematic models alongside G-protein-coupled receptor (GPCR)–PLC–PKC signaling cascades (`valicherla2018`, `garg2023_review`, `maj2025_fimmu`).

### Unknown
* **Sex differences in metabolic disease efficacy:** No published study has tested both male and female animals side-by-side in any diabetes, obesity, NASH, or steatosis disease model to test for a sex-by-treatment statistical interaction (`valicherla2018`, `valicherla2022_pk`, `tshikudi2024_colitis`).
* **Independent replication in metabolic models:** All published metabolic/T2D/PCOS/NASH preclinical studies of PSTi8 originate from a single research group (J.R. Gayen et al. at CSIR-CDRI), with only the ulcerative colitis/colonic epithelial cell studies conducted by an independent laboratory (J.-E. Ghia et al. at University of Manitoba) (`valicherla2018`, `yadav2025_pcos_dhea`, `tshikudi2024_colitis`).
* **Oral bioavailability and clinical translation:** Although in vitro solubility in simulated gastric and intestinal fluids was measured, in vivo oral bioavailability and human clinical trial data have not been reported in any indexed study (`valicherla2022_pk`, `christoffersson2026_fcdhc`).

## D. CONTRADICTIONS

* **`valicherla2022_pk` vs. `tshikudi2024_colitis` (and `tshikudi2024_jcag`):** `valicherla2022_pk` found no sex differences in systemic pharmacokinetic behavior of PSTi8 in healthy Sprague-Dawley rats, whereas `tshikudi2024_colitis` and `tshikudi2024_jcag` demonstrated sex-dependent biological effects of PSTi8 on colonic epithelial cell homeostasis and acute DSS colitis progression in C57BL/6 mice (beneficial delay of onset in males vs. elevated daily DAI at 72–96h in females).
* **Metadata contradiction in repository table vs. PubMed for `reza2022_lfs` (`10.1016/j.lfs.2021.120221`):** In `data/evidence_table.csv`, DOI `10.1016/j.lfs.2021.120221` is cited as *"Reza et al., Life Sciences, 2022"*, whereas verified bibliometric records show **Richa Garg** is first author of `10.1016/j.lfs.2021.120221` (*Garg R, Gupta AP, Katekar R, Verma S, Goand UK, Dadge S, Gayen JR, Life Sci 2022;289:120221*) and **Mohammad Irshad Reza** is first author of a separate uncatalogued PSTi8 paper in the same journal, `10.1016/j.lfs.2021.119905` (*Reza MI, Syed AA, Singh P, Husain A, Gayen JR, Life Sci 2021;284:119905*) (`reza2021_mito`, `reza2022_lfs`).
* **Metadata resolution for `dadge2023_nash` (`10.1016/j.cyto.2023.156354`):** In `data/evidence_table.csv`, animal sex was listed as `NOT_READ` from the abstract, whereas PubMed MeSH indexing for this exact record (PMID: 37690261) explicitly indexes `"Male"` C57BL mice (`dadge2023_nash`).

## E. OPEN QUESTIONS

* **[Inference]** Does PSTi8 exhibit sex-dimorphic efficacy or toxicity in metabolic syndrome (HFD, db/db, NASH) when males and females are compared directly in a factorial two-way ANOVA design, given that all non-reproductive metabolic studies used exclusively male mice (or left sex unstated in abstracts) while female rodents were restricted to ovariectomized/VCD menopausal or androgen/letrozole PCOS models?
* **[Inference]** Why did the effective chronic dose of PSTi8 shift from 0.3–2 mg/kg IP in mouse and rat T2D/menopause studies (`valicherla2018`, `gupta2019_dex`, `singh2020_heliyon`, `reza2022_lfs`) to 10 mg/kg in the 2025 DHEA+HFD rat PCOS study (`yadav2025_pcos_dhea`), especially considering that `valicherla2022_pk` reported non-linear pharmacokinetics in rats?
* **[Inference]** How does C-terminally truncated PSTi8 (PSTC$\Delta$8) compare head-to-head against N-terminally truncated PSTv1 (PSTN$\Delta$3) in receptor binding affinity and metabolic potency?
