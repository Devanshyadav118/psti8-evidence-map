C. KNOWLEDGE SUMMARY

Established
- GRP78/HSPA5 structure, post-translational modifications, and UPR regulation in insulin resistance: GRP78 (BiP/HSPA5) consists of an N-terminal nucleotide-binding/ATPase domain (NBD, sharing >90% homology across human, mouse, and rat) connected via a 10-amino-acid linker to a C-terminal substrate-binding domain ($\text{SBD}\beta$ hydrophobic pocket and $\text{SBD}\alpha$ helical lid) and a C-terminal KDEL ER-retention motif (`samanta2021_canres`, `li2026_theranostics`). Under homeostatic conditions, monomeric GRP78 binds and represses the ER transmembrane sensors PERK, IRE1$\alpha$, and ATF6; under ER stress in obesity and diabetes, misfolded proteins sequester GRP78, releasing IRE1$\alpha$ (which hyperactivates JNK to phosphorylate IRS-1 on inhibitory serine residues and splices XBP1 mRNA), PERK (which phosphorylates eIF2$\alpha$ to induce ATF4 and pro-apoptotic CHOP/GADD153), and ATF6 (`ozcan2004_science`, `samanta2021_canres`, `depaoli2025_jes`, `li2026_theranostics`). Eight reversible post-translational modifications regulate GRP78, including SBD phosphorylation (Thr453, Thr473, Thr500), hARTC1-mediated ADP-ribosylation (Arg470, Arg492, which reduces unfolded protein aggregation by 40%–65% and unnecessary degradation by 25.8%), METTL21A-mediated Lys585 trimethylation, UHRF1/GP78 Lys48-linked ubiquitination (counteracted by OTUD3), HDAC6-regulated Lys633 deacetylation, and competitive Cys420 ZDHHC9 S-palmitoylation versus $\text{H}_2\text{S}$-mediated S-sulfhydration (`li2026_theranostics`).
- GRP78 ATPase activity, small-molecule and peptide inhibitors, and cell-surface signaling: ATP binding to the GRP78 NBD stabilizes an open $\text{SBD}\alpha$ lid conformation with low substrate affinity, whereas ATP hydrolysis to ADP closes the lid to lock peptide substrates (`samanta2021_canres`, `li2026_theranostics`). Small-molecule and peptide inhibitors that suppress GRP78 ATPase activity include `VER-155008` ($\text{IC}_{50} = 0.7 \pm 0.3\ \mu\text{M}$, non-selective for HSP70/GRP78), the selective hydroxyquinoline `YUM70` ($\text{IC}_{50} = 1.5 \pm 0.3\ \mu\text{M}$, which docks to $\text{SBD}\beta$ residues Ser452, Gln458, Val461, Thr462, Ile463, and Lys464 with GOLD fitness score 61.17 vs. 54.57 for inactive analog YUM117, dissociating PERK and IRE1$\alpha$ without binding HSP70, PDI, or GSTO1), and pancreastatin (`IC50` $\sim 5.2\ \mu\text{M}$); in primary hepatocytes, inhibiting GRP78 ATPase activity with PST, `VER-155008`, or ADP increases `G6Pase` expression (`biswas2014_plosone`, `samanta2021_canres`, `li2026_theranostics`). Under ER stress, GRP78 also translocates to the cell surface (`sGRP78`/`csGRP78`, including via an IRE1$\alpha$–SRC–ASAP1 pathway), where it complexes with the p85 subunit of PI3K (disrupted by an N-terminal domain insertion mutation) to stimulate $\text{PI(3,4,5)P}_3$ production and Akt activation, and inhibits PP2A-mediated dephosphorylation of Akt at Thr308 and Ser473 (`zhang2013_plosone`, `li2026_theranostics`).
- Hepatic IRS1/2–PI3K–AKT–FoxO1–GSK3$\beta$ signaling and gluconeogenic genes (`PEPCK`, `G6Pase`): Insulin binding to the $\alpha_2\beta_2$ insulin receptor (`InsR`) induces $\beta$-subunit autophosphorylation (Tyr1146, Tyr1150, Tyr1151 in the catalytic loop; Tyr953/960 in the juxtamembrane domain; Tyr1316/1322 in the C-terminal tail), recruiting IRS-1 and IRS-2 to activate PI3K, PDK1 (which phosphorylates Akt at Thr308), and mTORC2 (which phosphorylates Akt at Ser473) (`dong2006_jci`, `cruzvillarreal2026_kp`). Activated Akt phosphorylates FoxO1 to exclude it from the nucleus, suppressing transcription of the rate-limiting gluconeogenic genes `Pck1` (`PEPCK`) and `G6pc` (`G6Pase`) (`dong2006_jci`, `gayen2009_jbc`, `zhu2014_ajpendo`). Combined hepatocyte deletion of `Irs1` and `Irs2` (`LKO::Irs1-/-` or `DKO`, which reduces mouse body size by 70% vs. WT and 40% vs. `Irs1-/-`) abolishes hepatic Akt–FoxO1 phosphorylation, elevates `Pck1`, `G6pc`, `Ppargc1`, and `Igfbp1`, and causes immediate neonatal diabetes (`dong2006_jci`). In `DKO` livers, unrestrained nuclear FoxO1 also transcriptionally upregulates heme oxygenase-1 (`Hmox1`), which disrupts mitochondrial respiratory complexes III and IV and lowers the $\text{NAD}^+/\text{NADH}$ ratio and ATP production, defects rescued by hepatic `Foxo1` deletion (`cheng2009_natmed`). By contrast, hepatic GSK-3$\beta$ Ser9 phosphorylation and glycogen stores remain nearly normal in `LKO::Irs1-/-` mice (`dong2006_jci`), and liver-specific `Gsk3b` knockout mice exhibit normal glucose/insulin tolerance and insulin signaling (`patel2008_mcb`).
- PKC isoforms ($\text{PKC}\lambda/\zeta$, $\text{PKC}\varepsilon$, $\text{PKC}\delta$, cPKC) and SREBP-1c in selective hepatic insulin resistance: Downstream of PI3K, atypical $\text{PKC}\lambda/\iota$ and $\text{PKC}\zeta$ (activated by $\text{PIP}_3$ binding to their pseudosubstrate domain and PDK1/mTORC2 phosphorylation) promote GLUT4 translocation in skeletal muscle and adipocytes, but in liver $\text{PKC}\lambda$ mediates insulin- and PI3K-induced `Srebp1c` and `Fas` expression (`matsumoto2003_jci`, `cruzvillarreal2026_kp`). Male liver-specific $\text{PKC}\lambda$ knockout (`L-lambdaKO`) mice display reduced refeeding-induced hepatic `Srebp1c` and `Fas` mRNA, lower hepatic triglyceride content, and enhanced whole-body insulin sensitivity without altered `Pck1` or `G6pc` suppression (`matsumoto2003_jci`). Conversely, intracellular diacylglycerol (DAG) activates novel $\text{PKC}\varepsilon$, which phosphorylates human `InsR` at Thr1160 in the catalytic loop to inhibit `InsR` tyrosine kinase activity (`nasce2022_antiox`, `alves2025_pathophys`, `cruzvillarreal2026_kp`); conventional $\text{PKC}\alpha/\beta$ and atypical $\text{PKC}\zeta$ phosphorylate IRS-1 on inhibitory serine residues (including Ser323/318 and Ser498/570 by $\text{PKC}\zeta$) (`cruzvillarreal2026_kp`); and hepatic $\text{PKC}\delta$ promotes lipogenesis via SREBP-1c, FAS, and ACC (`cruzvillarreal2026_kp`).
- Oxidative stress, NOX3, and mitochondrial ROS in JNK-mediated hepatic insulin resistance: Saturated fatty acids (palmitate, but not monounsaturated oleate) and $\text{TNF-}\alpha$ stimulate hepatocyte ROS production that activates JNK and p38MAPK, inhibiting IRS-1 and IRS-2 tyrosine phosphorylation, Akt and GSK phosphorylation, and glycogen synthesis while increasing gluconeogenesis (`nakamura2009_jbc`, `li2010_febslett`, `gao2010_jbc`, `nasce2022_antiox`). While `nakamura2009_jbc` demonstrated in rat H4IIEC3 hepatocytes that palmitate-induced ROS and JNK activation arise from CPT-1-dependent mitochondrial $\beta$-oxidation electron flux, `li2010_febslett` and `gao2010_jbc` showed in HepG2 cells and *db/db* mouse livers that $\text{TNF-}\alpha$ and palmitate upregulate NADPH oxidase 3 (`NOX3`), and that `NOX3` siRNA prevents ROS generation, JNK/p38MAPK activation, and glycogen loss.
- AMPK activation and $G\alpha_{q/11}$–$\text{PLC}\beta$–PKC counter-regulation in liver: AMPK directly binds and phosphorylates SREBP-1c at Ser372 (and SREBP-2) in response to metformin and polyphenols (`S17834`), blocking SREBP-1c proteolytic cleavage and nuclear translocation to suppress lipogenesis and hepatic steatosis (`li2011_cmet`), while also promoting ACC phosphorylation to inhibit malonyl-CoA synthesis and enhance CPT1A-mediated fatty acid oxidation (`nasce2022_antiox`, `li2026_theranostics`). Conversely, activation of hepatocyte $G\alpha_{q/11}$-coupled GPCRs stimulates $\text{PLC}\beta$ to hydrolyze $\text{PIP}_2$ into $\text{IP}_3$ (triggering ER $\text{Ca}^{2+}$ release) and DAG (`santosalvarez1998_mce`, `li2013_endocrinology`, `cruzvillarreal2026_kp`), which stimulates glycogenolysis and gluconeogenesis in vivo (`li2013_endocrinology`).
- Sex hormones, estrogen receptor-$\alpha$ ($\text{ER}\alpha$), ER stress, and AMPK/SREBP-1c in females and males: Ovariectomy (OVX) in female Sprague-Dawley rats increases liver triacylglycerol by 51% ($21.9 \pm 2.6$ vs. $14.5 \pm 1.2\text{ mg/g}$, $P < 0.01$), decreases hepatic `PPARalpha` mRNA by 66%, and increases hepatic `SREBP-1c` and `SCD-1` transcripts by 80% and 41%, all of which are prevented by $17\beta$-estradiol ($\text{E}_2$) replacement (`paquette2008_menopause`). In sexually dimorphic TALLYHO/JngJ mice, males develop chronic hyperglycemia by 5 weeks ($27.55\text{ mmol/L}$ vs. $7.9\text{ mmol/L}$ in intact females), and OVX in females induces chronic hyperglycemia ($>15\text{ mM}$ by 12 weeks) and hypoinsulinemia ($0.35 \pm 0.15$ vs. $0.90 \pm 0.16\text{ ng/mL}$ in sham) accompanied by elevated islet GRP78/GRP94 and pro-apoptotic GADD153/CHOP, which is partially alleviated by the chemical chaperone 4-PBA ($1\text{ g/kg/day}$) (`depaoli2025_jes`). Centrally, OVX in female rats increases 15-day weight gain ($48.63 \pm 2.38\text{ g}$ vs. $12.7 \pm 2.54\text{ g}$ in sham) and mediobasal hypothalamic ceramide-induced ER stress (`pIRE1`, `pPERK`, `peIF2alpha`, `CHOP`); intracerebroventricular $\text{E}_2$ or adenoviral GRP78 overexpression in the ventromedial hypothalamus (VMH)—where $\text{E}_2$ also acts via VMH AMPK—relieves ER stress, activates sympathetic $\beta_3$-AR brown adipose thermogenesis, and reduces hepatic steatosis (`gonzalezgarcia2018_cellrep`). Peripherally, hepatocyte $\text{ER}\alpha$ is required in HFD-fed male mice to maintain clamp suppression of endogenous glucose production, insulin-stimulated Akt-Ser473 phosphorylation, nuclear FOXO1 exclusion, and ACC phosphorylation (`zhu2014_ajpendo`), while AAV8-TBG hepatic $\text{ER}\alpha$ overexpression in male C57BL/6J mice reduces body fat by 28%, increases primary hepatocyte basal (19%, $141 \pm 3.1$ vs. $118 \pm 5.3$) and maximal (34%, $315 \pm 11.6$ vs. $235 \pm 13.4$) oxygen consumption, lowers HFD hepatic triglycerides by 43%, and improves hepatic insulin sensitivity by 145% (`alves2025_pathophys`).

Contested
- Pancreastatin signaling via $G\alpha_{11}$–$\text{PLC}\beta_3$–cPKC–eNOS vs. direct luminal/cell-surface GRP78 binding (single-lab series per mechanism): Using blocking antisera in male Wistar rat liver membranes, `Santos-Alvarez & Sanchez-Margalet` (`santosalvarez1998_mce`, single-lab finding) demonstrated that PST selectively activates $\text{PLC}\beta_3$ via pertussis-toxin-insensitive $G\alpha_{11}$ (and not $G\alpha_q$, $\text{PLC}\beta_1$, $\text{PLC}\beta_2$, or $\text{PLC}\beta_4$). Subsequently, the UCSD/CDRI group showed in `gayen2009_jbc` (single-lab finding) that PST activates conventional PKC and eNOS/NO to induce `G6Pase` transcription and suppress IRS1/2–PI3K–Akt–FoxO1 and SREBP-1c maturation in `Chga-KO` mice, and in `biswas2014_plosone` (single-lab finding) identified ER chaperone GRP78 as a pH-dependent hepatic PST binding partner whose ATPase activity is inhibited by PST ($\text{IC}_{50} \sim 5.2\ \mu\text{M}$). How extracellular PST crosses or signals across the hepatocyte plasma membrane to engage luminal versus cell-surface GRP78 alongside $G\alpha_{11}$–$\text{PLC}\beta_3$ remains unresolved (`santosalvarez1998_mce`, `gayen2009_jbc`, `biswas2014_plosone`).
- Effects of GRP78 overexpression vs. `Grp78(+/-)` haploinsufficiency and circulating GRP78 on insulin sensitivity: Adenoviral GRP78 overexpression in *ob/ob* mouse liver (`kammoun2009_jci`, single-lab study in liver) or in the VMH of ovariectomized female rats (`gonzalezgarcia2018_cellrep`) reduces ER stress, inhibits SREBP-1c cleavage, reduces hepatic steatosis, and improves insulin sensitivity, and GRP78 overexpression in primary hepatocytes inhibits `G6Pase` expression (`biswas2014_plosone`). Paradoxically, whole-body `Grp78(+/-)` haploinsufficiency in male HFD-fed mice (`ye2010_diabetes`, single-lab study) also protects against diet-induced obesity, hepatic steatosis, and insulin resistance by triggering compensatory adaptive UPR chaperones (ATF6, EDEM) in white adipose tissue, whereas continuous systemic infusion of recombinant GRP78 and HSP70 in chow-fed rats induces hepatic and whole-body insulin resistance, hyperglycemia, and NASH that is reversed by anti-GRP78 monoclonal antibodies (`angelini2022_natcommun`, single-lab study).
- PI3K–$\text{PKC}\lambda$ vs. PI3K–Akt–mTORC1/`Insig2a` and non-enzymatic ABHD6 in selective hepatic insulin resistance: While `matsumoto2003_jci` showed that hepatocyte $\text{PKC}\lambda$ deletion reduces refeeding- and PI3K-induced `Srebp1c` expression and hepatic triglycerides in male mice, `yecies2011_cmet` (single-lab study in `LTsc1KO` mice) demonstrated that constitutive mTORC1 activation cannot induce hepatic SREBP-1c when negative feedback attenuates Akt, because Akt is required to suppress the SREBP-1c inhibitor `Insig2a`. Furthermore, a 2026 preprint (`li2026_biorxiv`, single-lab study) identified nuclear translocation of non-enzymatic ABHD6 in obese and aged male mice as a physical disruptor of the Akt–FoxO1 complex that drives selective hepatic insulin resistance and MASLD.
- Requirement of hepatocyte $\text{ER}\alpha$ (`LERKO`) for $\text{E}_2$-mediated reduction of post-ovariectomy hepatic steatosis: In developmental `LERKO` ovariectomized female mice given $\text{E}_2$ at the time of OVX (`zhu2013_diabetes`), hepatocyte $\text{ER}\alpha$ deletion abolished $\text{E}_2$'s ability to reduce liver lipid deposition and improve clamp hepatic insulin sensitivity. In contrast, a 2026 bioRxiv preprint (`franczak2026_biorxiv`, single-lab study) using adult-onset AAV-Cre `LERKO` in middle-aged (36–40-week-old) ovariectomized female mice given $\text{E}_2$ after 6 weeks of HFD found that $\text{E}_2$ significantly lowered hepatic triglycerides, de novo lipogenesis proteins, and mitochondrial $\text{H}_2\text{O}_2$ emission in both control and `LERKO` mice, although `LERKO` mice retained higher absolute hepatic triglycerides and completely failed to increase palmitoyl-CoA-supported basal and State 3 mitochondrial respiration in response to $\text{E}_2$.
- Hepatocyte NOX3 expression in HepG2 cells vs. primary mammalian hepatocytes: Although `li2010_febslett` and `gao2010_jbc` (single-lab series by Jian Li's group) demonstrated that `NOX3` siRNA rescues $\text{TNF-}\alpha$- and palmitate-induced ROS, JNK/p38MAPK activation, and gluconeogenesis in human HepG2 hepatoma cells and *db/db* mouse livers, `nasce2022_antiox` notes that while NOX1, NOX2, NOX4, DUOX1, and DUOX2 are established in primary hepatocytes, `NOX3` expression in primary hepatocytes outside the HepG2 cell line remains undetermined.

Unknown
- Atomic-level 3D structures of post-translationally modified GRP78: High-resolution cryo-EM, X-ray crystallographic, or HDX-MS structures of full-length GRP78 carrying site-specific modifications (Thr453/473/500 phosphorylation, Arg470/492 ADP-ribosylation, or competing Cys420 S-palmitoylation vs. S-sulfhydration) have not yet been determined (`li2026_theranostics`).
- Molecular bridge between cell-surface $G\alpha_{11}$–$\text{PLC}\beta_3$ activation and luminal/surface GRP78 ATPase inhibition by pancreastatin: No study has yet tested whether PST binds cell-surface translocated GRP78 (`sGRP78`) on hepatocytes or whether $G\alpha_{11}$–$\text{PLC}\beta_3$ $\text{Ca}^{2+}$ mobilization and GRP78 ATPase inhibition operate in parallel or in series (`santosalvarez1998_mce`, `biswas2014_plosone`, `li2026_theranostics`).
- Direct sex-by-treatment comparisons in GRP78-ATPase, NOX3-JNK, and hepatocyte $G_q$ signaling studies: None of the primary studies on PST–GRP78 binding (`biswas2014_plosone`), hepatic GRP78 overexpression (`kammoun2009_jci`), `Grp78(+/-)` haploinsufficiency (`ye2010_diabetes`), or NOX3–JNK signaling (`li2010_febslett`, `gao2010_jbc`) tested both male and female animals side-by-side to evaluate sex interactions.

D. CONTRADICTIONS

- Hepatic GRP78 overexpression (`kammoun2009_jci`, `biswas2014_plosone`) vs. `Grp78(+/-)` heterozygosity (`ye2010_diabetes`) and systemic GRP78 infusion (`angelini2022_natcommun`): `kammoun2009_jci` and `biswas2014_plosone` showed that hepatic GRP78 overexpression suppresses SREBP-1c cleavage and `G6Pase` expression and reverses steatosis and insulin resistance in *ob/ob* mice, whereas `ye2010_diabetes` showed that reducing GRP78 gene dosage by 50% (`Grp78+/-`) also protects against HFD-induced steatosis and insulin resistance via adaptive UPR hormesis in white adipose tissue, and `angelini2022_natcommun` showed that infusing circulating recombinant GRP78 induces hepatic insulin resistance, hyperglycemia, and NASH.
- Hepatocyte $\text{ER}\alpha$ requirement for $\text{E}_2$-mediated triglyceride reduction in ovariectomized female mice (`zhu2013_diabetes` vs. `franczak2026_biorxiv`): `zhu2013_diabetes` reported that developmental liver $\text{ER}\alpha$ knockout (`LERKO`) mice failed to reduce liver lipid accumulation when treated with $\text{E}_2$ at the time of ovariectomy, whereas `franczak2026_biorxiv` found that middle-aged inducible AAV-Cre `LERKO` mice treated with $\text{E}_2$ 6 weeks post-OVX substantially reduced hepatic triglycerides and de novo lipogenesis proteins independently of hepatocyte $\text{ER}\alpha$ (while requiring hepatocyte $\text{ER}\alpha$ strictly for $\text{E}_2$-stimulated palmitate-supported mitochondrial respiration).
- Predominant ROS source in palmitate-exposed hepatocytes (`nakamura2009_jbc` vs. `gao2010_jbc` and `nasce2022_antiox`): `gao2010_jbc` concluded that `NOX3` is the predominant source of palmitate-induced ROS driving JNK activation in HepG2 cells, whereas `nakamura2009_jbc` demonstrated in rat H4IIEC3 hepatocytes that palmitate-induced ROS and JNK activation are driven by CPT-1-dependent mitochondrial $\beta$-oxidation electron transport chain flux, and `nasce2022_antiox` noted that `NOX3` expression in primary hepatocytes outside HepG2 cells remains unconfirmed.

E. OPEN QUESTIONS

- [Inference] Why does inhibiting GRP78 ATPase activity with small molecules (`YUM70`, $\text{IC}_{50} = 1.5\ \mu\text{M}$; `VER-155008`, $\text{IC}_{50} = 0.7\ \mu\text{M}$) or pancreastatin ($\text{IC}_{50} \sim 5.2\ \mu\text{M}$) dissociate PERK/IRE1$\alpha$, induce CHOP, or upregulate `G6Pase` (`biswas2014_plosone`, `samanta2021_canres`), whereas competitive antagonism of PST at GRP78 by PSTi8 enhances GRP78 expression/activity and AMPK signaling while suppressing `G6Pase`?
- [Inference] Since `Cys420` of GRP78 is a competitive switch between ZDHHC9-mediated S-palmitoylation (which stabilizes ER-retained GRP78 and inhibits UPR sensors) and $\text{H}_2\text{S}$-mediated S-sulfhydration (which dissociates IRE1$\alpha$) (`li2026_theranostics`), and hyperglycemia reduces GRP78 S-nitrosylation via ROS (`li2026_theranostics`), does NOX3- or PST/eNOS-derived reactive nitrogen/oxygen signaling (`gayen2009_jbc`, `gao2010_jbc`) alter GRP78 post-translational modifications in insulin-resistant liver?
- [Inference] Given that ovariectomy in female rodents simultaneously triggers hypothalamic ceramide-induced ER stress (`gonzalezgarcia2018_cellrep`), pancreatic islet GRP78/CHOP upregulation (`depaoli2025_jes`), hepatic SREBP-1c upregulation (`paquette2008_menopause`), and loss of $\text{ER}\alpha$-dependent hepatic mitochondrial fatty acid oxidation (`franczak2026_biorxiv`), does estrogen status modify the dose-response or signaling efficacy of GRP78/AMPK-targeting peptides in intact cycling versus ovariectomized females?
