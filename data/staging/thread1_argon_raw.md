## C. KNOWLEDGE SUMMARY

### Established
* **Proteolytic processing of CHGA into pancreastatin:** Chromogranin A (human 439-aa acidic secretory glycoprotein encoded on chromosome 14) contains multiple paired and single basic cleavage sites and is proteolytically processed inside neuroendocrine secretory granules by prohormone convertase-1 (PC1/PC3) and prohormone convertase-2 (PC2), followed by C-terminal amidation (`arden1994_biochemj`, `eskeland1996_jci`, `udupi1999_regulpept`, `laslop2002_regulpept`, `ioannidis2022_peptides`). In 2025, inflammatory macrophages were also shown to express proteases that cleave extracellular CgA depending on their polarization state (`ioannidis2025_jes`).
* **Pancreastatin sequence, molecular forms, and species differences:** First isolated from porcine pancreas as a 49-amino-acid C-terminally glycine-amidated peptide (`tatemoto1986_nature`), human PST corresponds to `CHGA(250-301)` (52-mer, the predominant form in normal human plasma alongside 29-mer `CHGA(273-301)`, 48-mer `CHGA(254-301)`, and larger fragments such as tumor-derived 92-aa `hCgA(210-301)`), whereas murine PST corresponds to `mCgA(263-314)` (`schmidt1988_pnas`, `kitayama1994_lifesci`, `allu2022_diabetes`, `ioannidis2022_peptides`). Its C-terminal region carries the core biological activity (`efendic1987_pnas`, `allu2014_jbc`).
* **Plasma PST and CHGA levels in T2D, obesity, insulin resistance, and NETs:** Plasma PST circulates at picomolar levels (~5–30 pM) in healthy subjects (`sanchezmargalet1998_diacare`, `odorisio2010_pancreas`). In T2D, fasting PST is elevated ~3.7-fold in obese T2D patients compared with healthy controls or non-diabetic obese subjects (and does not fall after substantial weight loss) (`oconnor2005_jcem`, `bandyopadhyay2017_fendo`), and oral glucose ingestion triggers a significant postprandial PST surge in T2D patients (`funakoshi1990_regulpept`). In neuroendocrine tumors (NETs), plasma PST and CgA are markedly elevated and predict hepatic metastasis and reduced survival (`odorisio2010_pancreas`, `rustagi2013_jso`, `sherman2014_aso`).
* **Human CHGA genetic variants:** Resequencing identified non-synonymous variants in the PST domain including `Arg253Trp`, `Ala256Gly`, `Glu287Lys` (`E287K`), and `Gly297Ser` (`G297S`, `rs9658664`) (`oconnor2005_jcem`, `allu2014_jbc`). The C-terminal `Gly297Ser` variant (and `Glu287Lys`, studied primarily by the Mahapatra and O'Connor laboratories) increases peptide $\alpha$-helical propensity, displays higher binding affinity for GRP78 and the insulin receptor (`INSR`), potently inhibits insulin-stimulated glucose uptake (`PST-297S > PST-287K > PST-WT`), increases gluconeogenic gene expression, and confers a 1.3- to 1.6-fold increased risk of T2D, hypertension, metabolic syndrome, and coronary artery disease in a 4,300-subject Indian cohort (`oconnor2005_jcem`, `allu2014_jbc`, `allu2022_diabetes`). Non-coding CHGA variants including promoter haplotype 2 (`-1018T/-57T`, activated by c-Rel; single-lab finding, Mahapatra lab), `T-415C` (`rs9658635`), and 5'-UTR `rs9658628-A` are also associated with higher circulating CHGA, fasting glucose, and insulin resistance (`subramanian2017_jbc`, `marjani2024_jafes`, `crocco2025_fragi`).
* **`Chga` knockout (`Chga-KO`) and `PSTv1` mouse work:** Systemic `Chga-KO` mice (developed and characterized primarily by the Mahata/O'Connor/Gayen and Christoffersson groups) are hypertensive and hyperadrenergic yet maintain euglycemia and resist diet-induced obesity (DIO) insulin resistance due to heightened hepatic insulin sensitivity, suppressed hepatic `Pepck` and `G6pase`, and reduced adipose tissue macrophage inflammation (`gayen2009_jbc`, `bandyopadhyay2015_diabetes`, `muntjewerff2025_commbiol`). Exogenous PST administration in `Chga-KO` mice reverses this protection and restores insulin resistance (`gayen2009_jbc`, `bandyopadhyay2015_diabetes`, `muntjewerff2025_commbiol`). Conversely, the N-terminally truncated peptide variant `PSTv1` (`PST-NDelta3`, `hCHGA276-301`, lacking 3 N-terminal residues; single-lab finding, Mahata lab) acts as a competitive antagonist that mimics the `Chga-KO` phenotype and sensitizes wild-type DIO mice to insulin (`bandyopadhyay2015_diabetes`, `ioannidis2022_peptides`).
* **Measurement methods (RIA, ELISA, mass spectrometry):** Circulating CgA and PST are measured by radioimmunoassay (RIA), ELISA, and mass spectrometry (`odorisio2010_pancreas`, `rustagi2013_jso`, `marjani2024_jafes`, `muntjewerff2025_commbiol`). Carboxy-terminal-specific human PST RIAs avoid cross-reactivity with intact CgA (`odorisio2010_pancreas`). Crucially, unlike intact CgA—which is falsely elevated by proton pump inhibitor (PPI)-induced enterochromaffin-like (ECL) cell hyperplasia—plasma PST levels are not altered by chronic PPI therapy (`raines2012_pancreas`).
* **Actions on liver, adipose tissue, skeletal muscle, and pancreatic islets:**
  * *Islets:* PST inhibits first- and second-phase glucose- and arginine-stimulated insulin secretion and stimulates glucagon release (`tatemoto1986_nature`, `efendic1987_pnas`). `Chga-KO` mice have reduced total beta-cell mass (`muntjewerff2025_commbiol`) but exhibit increased mature membrane-docked insulin granules, enhanced beta-cell mitochondrial volume and fusion, and elevated glucose-stimulated insulin secretion (`wollam2017_ctr`, single-lab finding, Mahata lab).
  * *Liver:* PST binds GRP78 (inhibiting its ATPase activity) and activates a $G\alpha_{q/11}$–PLC$\beta_3$–$\text{Ca}^{2+}$–PKC and nitric oxide synthase (NOS) pathway that suppresses IRS1/2–PI3K–Akt–FoxO1 signaling and SREBP-1c maturation, stimulating glycogenolysis and gluconeogenic transcription (`PEPCK`, `G6Pase`) (`gayen2009_jbc`, `valicherla2013_physiolgen`, `biswas2014_plosone`).
  * *Adipose tissue:* PST inhibits insulin-stimulated glucose uptake, glycogen synthesis (via PI3K/PKC and GSK-3 activation), and lipogenesis while stimulating lipolysis and pro-inflammatory macrophage cytokine expression (`sanchezmargalet1998_ajp`, `valicherla2013_physiolgen`, `bandyopadhyay2015_diabetes`).
  * *Skeletal muscle:* In humans, brachial artery infusion of PST (~200 nM) directly blunts forearm skeletal muscle glucose uptake by ~50% (`oconnor2005_jcem`).

### Contested
* **Fasting vs. postprandial plasma PST and total CgA elevations in T2D and GDM:** Earlier RIA studies in non-obese NIDDM found no difference in fasting PST compared to healthy controls, observing an elevation only after an oral glucose load (`funakoshi1990_regulpept`), whereas later work found ~3.7-fold elevated fasting PST in obese T2D subjects (`oconnor2005_jcem`). Similarly, while `sanchezmargalet1998_diacare` reported significantly elevated fasting PST in late-pregnancy GDM, `unalkocabas2024_obstetmed` found no significant difference in mean plasma PST between GDM and non-GDM pregnant women tested at earlier gestational weeks, even though PST correlated with HOMA-IR within the GDM group. Total serum CgA in T2D is likewise contested: some studies report elevated serum and salivary CgA associated with poor glycemic control, whereas `herold2021_wjd` notes that most T2D patients have normal serum CgA (<98.1 ng/mL).
* **Direct pro-inflammatory action of PST on macrophages across species:** In mouse models (`Chga-KO` DIO and DSS colitis), PST strongly promotes M1 macrophage pro-inflammatory cytokine expression (`IL-6`, `TNF-alpha`, `IL-18`, `MCP-1`) and suppresses alternatively activated M2 macrophages (`bandyopadhyay2015_diabetes`, `ioannidis2022_peptides`, `ioannidis2025_jes`). However, a 2025 study in primary human monocyte-derived macrophages found that exogenous PST and CST had only minor direct effects on human macrophage cytokine production and immunometabolism (`ioannidis2025_jes`).
* **Skeletal muscle insulin sensitivity in `Chga-KO` mice on chow vs. high-fat diet:** Under basal chow-fed conditions, `Chga-KO` mice exhibit skeletal muscle insulin resistance alongside hepatic insulin hypersensitivity (`gayen2009_jbc`, `bandyopadhyay2017_fendo`), whereas on a high-fat diet (`KO-DIO`), `Chga-KO` mice show enhanced Akt and AMPK signaling in both skeletal muscle and white adipose tissue compared with wild-type DIO controls (`bandyopadhyay2015_diabetes`).

### Unknown
* **Identity of the canonical cell-surface PST receptor:** Although PST has been cross-linked to an ~80 kDa membrane glycoprotein coupled to $G\alpha_{q/11}$ and shown to bind the endoplasmic reticulum/cell-surface chaperone GRP78 (`valicherla2013_physiolgen`, `biswas2014_plosone`) and insulin receptor in silico/in vitro (`allu2022_diabetes`), a dedicated, cloned cell-surface G-protein-coupled receptor for PST on metabolic or immune cells remains unidentified (`biswas2014_plosone`, `ioannidis2022_peptides`).
* **Causal role of PST in initiating vs. amplifying human insulin resistance:** Because normotensive hyperinsulinemic offspring of insulin-resistant hypertensive parents have normal plasma PST and catecholamine levels prior to overt disease (`sanchezmargalet1997_joe`) and simple obesity without diabetes does not elevate PST (`oconnor2005_jcem`), whether endogenous PST elevation initiates human insulin resistance or arises secondary to established metabolic decompensation remains unresolved (`sanchezmargalet1997_joe`, `unalkocabas2024_obstetmed`).

## D. CONTRADICTIONS

* **Mean plasma PST in gestational diabetes mellitus (`sanchezmargalet1998_diacare` vs. `unalkocabas2024_obstetmed`):** `sanchezmargalet1998_diacare` reported significantly higher fasting plasma PST-like immunoreactivity in GDM patients compared with healthy pregnant controls (46 ± 2 vs. 30 ± 2 pmol/L at ~30 weeks gestation), whereas `unalkocabas2024_obstetmed` found no statistically significant difference in plasma PST levels between GDM and non-GDM pregnant women (attributing the discrepancy to earlier gestational week of testing and lower IADPSG glycemic thresholds).
* **Direct macrophage activation by PST in mice vs. humans (`bandyopadhyay2015_diabetes` vs. `ioannidis2025_jes`):** `bandyopadhyay2015_diabetes` (and murine colitis studies reviewed in `ioannidis2022_peptides`) demonstrated pronounced direct pro-inflammatory cytokine induction by PST in murine macrophages, whereas `ioannidis2025_jes` found that PST and CST exert only minor direct effects on cytokine production and metabolic reprogramming in human monocyte-derived macrophages.
* **Chow-fed vs. DIO skeletal muscle insulin sensitivity in `Chga-KO` mice (`gayen2009_jbc` vs. `bandyopadhyay2015_diabetes`):** `gayen2009_jbc` reported decreased insulin-stimulated glucose disposal (muscle insulin resistance) in chow-fed `Chga-KO` mice during hyperinsulinemic-euglycemic clamps (later attributed to CST deficiency in `bandyopadhyay2017_fendo`), whereas `bandyopadhyay2015_diabetes` reported enhanced Akt and AMPK insulin signaling in skeletal muscle of high-fat-diet-fed `Chga-KO` (`KO-DIO`) mice relative to WT-DIO mice.

## E. OPEN QUESTIONS

* **[Inference]** Since `Gly297Ser` (`PST-297S`) increases human PST $\alpha$-helicity and binding affinity to both GRP78 and the insulin receptor (`allu2014_jbc`, `allu2022_diabetes`), does competitive inhibition by C-terminally truncated PSTi8 or N-terminally truncated PSTv1 retain full potency in carriers of the `297Ser` allele, or are higher inhibitor concentrations required?
* **[Inference]** Given that human macrophages do not synthesize `CHGA` mRNA/protein but differentially express CgA-cleaving proteases (`ioannidis2025_jes`), to what extent is local adipose and hepatic PST in human obesity generated extracellularly by infiltrating macrophage proteases cleaving circulating full-length CgA versus secreted directly by neuroendocrine cells?
* **[Inference]** Why do plasma PST levels remain elevated after substantial weight loss in obese T2D patients (`oconnor2005_jcem`, `unalkocabas2024_obstetmed`), and could genetic promoter variants (`subramanian2017_jbc`, `crocco2025_fragi`) or islet/enterochromaffin hypersecretion explain persistent hyperpancreastatinemia?
