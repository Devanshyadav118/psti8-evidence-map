# Study Guide — PSTi8 Literature

This file catalogues all known published studies involving PSTi8
(pancreastatin inhibitor peptide, PEGKGEQEHSQQKEEEEEMAV-NH₂).

---

## Section 1 — Background

Pancreastatin (PST) is a chromogranin-A-derived peptide that inhibits
insulin secretion and promotes insulin resistance. PSTi8 is a 21-amino-acid
counter-regulatory peptide developed at CSIR-CDRI (India) and subsequently
studied by groups in Canada for colitis models.
