### B. DISCREPANCIES & NUANCES

1. **Precursor vs. Mature Numbering Offset ($+18$ in Human CHGA/GRP78, $+19$ in Mouse GRP78):**
   * **Human CHGA (`P10645`)** has an 18-residue signal peptide (`MRSAAVLALLLCAGQVTA`, residues `1–18`). Consequently, all mature CHGA coordinates are offset by **$-18$** relative to the canonical UniProtKB sequence (`457 aa` precursor vs. `439 aa` mature):
     * Full-length 52-aa human PST: Mature `CHGA(250–301)` = Precursor `P10645(268–319)`.
     * Bioactive 29-aa human PST (PST-29): Mature `CHGA(273–301)` = Precursor `P10645(291–319)`.
     * 21-aa inhibitor **PSTi8**: Mature `CHGA(273–293)` = Precursor `P10645(291–311)` (residues `1–21` of PST-29).
     * Cleaved C-terminal 8-mer **`VPQGLFRG`**: Mature `CHGA(294–301)` = Precursor `P10645(312–319)` (residues `22–29` of PST-29).
   * **Cross-species GRP78 numbering trap (`P20029` vs. `P11021`/`P06761`):** While Human (`P11021`) and Rat (`P06761`) GRP78 are `654 aa` precursors with an `18-aa` signal peptide (`1–18`, mature `19–654` = `636 aa`), **Mouse GRP78 (`P20029`) is `655 aa` long** because its N-terminal signal peptide is `19 aa` (`1–19`, mature `20–655` = `636 aa`). Thus, every functional site in mouse `P20029` is shifted by **$+1$** in UniProt precursor numbering (e.g., the catalytic Thr229 in human `P11021` is Thr230 in mouse `P20029`; the NBD annotation `125–280` in `P11021` is `126–281` in `P20029`).

2. **dbSNP rsID Discrepancy (`rs9658664` vs. `rs9658667`) and Variant Localization Relative to PSTi8:**
   * **True rsID of `Gly297Ser`:** In UniProtKB `P10645` (`VAR_025640`, `p.Gly315Ser` in precursor numbering / `Gly297Ser` in mature CHGA numbering), the verified dbSNP identifier is **`rs9658664`**. By contrast, **`rs9658667`** corresponds to `VAR_025643` (`p.Gly382Ser` in precursor `P10645` / `Gly364Ser` in mature CHGA), which is the well-known **Catestatin** variant, not Pancreastatin.
   * **Localization of `Gly297Ser` (`rs9658664`) vs. `Glu287Lys` (`rs140089514`):**
     $$\underbrace{\text{P}_{273}\text{EGKGEQEHSQQKE}\mathbf{\underline{\text{E}_{287}}}\text{EEEMAV}_{293}}_{\text{PSTi8 (21-aa, Precursor 291–311)}} - \underbrace{\text{V}_{294}\text{PQ}\mathbf{\underline{\text{G}_{297}}}\text{LFRG}_{301}}_{\text{Cleaved 8-mer (Precursor 312–319)}}$$
     * **`Gly297Ser` (`p.Gly315Ser`, `rs9658664`)** sits at position 25 of PST-29 (position 4 of `VPQGLFRG`), meaning it lies **exclusively inside the cleaved C-terminal octapeptide `VPQGLFRG`** and is **absent from PSTi8**.
     * **`Glu287Lys` (`p.Glu305Lys`, `rs140089514`)** sits at position 15 of PST-29 (the second glutamate of the `EEEEEMAV` motif), meaning it lies **directly inside the PSTi8 sequence**.

3. **Hidden Construct Modifications in RCSB PDB `5E84`, `5E85`, and `3LDL`:**
   * **`PDB 5E84` (Engineered `T229A` + `L3,4'` loop deletion):** Even though RCSB PDB's metadata summary displays `Mutation(s): No` (`Mutation(s): 0`) because the depositor submitted the engineered sequence as the reference entity, the primary crystallographic paper (*Yang et al., Structure 2015*, PMC4680848) documents that `5E84` was solved using the **`BiP-T229A-L3,4'` construct**:
     1. It contains the **`T229A`** point mutation in the NBD active site to block ATP hydrolysis.
     2. In the SBD$\beta$ substrate-binding cleft, the 7-residue loop **$L_{3,4}$ (`TASDNQP`, residues 457–463 of human BiP)**—which normally forms half of the polypeptide-binding pocket and contains key drug-binding residues (`Gln458`, `Val461`, `Thr462`)—was **deleted and replaced with a 3-residue `VGG` sequence (`L3,4'`)** to prevent BiP self-oligomerization during crystallization. This shortens the `25–633` construct from 609 to **606 residues** and **abolishes peptide binding to SBD$\beta$**.
     3. High $\text{Zn}^{2+}$ in the crystallization mother liquor caused **`ZN` ($\text{Zn}^{2+}$) to replace $\text{Mg}^{2+}$** at the ATPase active site in multiple chains.
   * **`PDB 5E85` (Covalently Tethered C-terminal `Tev-NR` Peptide):** `5E85` contains the wild-type human SBD sequence (`418–637`, 220 aa, unlike `5E86` which has the `L3,4'` mutation), but its deposited Chain A is **236 residues long** because a **9-aa TEV protease linker (`SENLYFQGS`) and 7-aa substrate peptide (`NRLLLTG`)** are covalently fused to residue 637. The fused `NRLLLTG` tail loops back into the SBD$\beta$ cleft in *cis* under the closed SBD$\alpha$ lid.
   * **`PDB 3LDL` (Asymmetric $\text{Mg}^{2+}$ Occupancy):** In `3LDL` (`2.30 Å`, human NBD `26–407`), both Chain A and Chain B contain bound `ATP`, but **only Chain B contains a modeled $\text{Mg}^{2+}$ ion (`MG`)**, whereas Chain A is metal-free. By contrast, **`PDB 5EVZ` (`1.85 Å`)** has wild-type sequence (`26–407`) with `ADP` + `PO4` + `MG` fully resolved in both chains.

### C. BIOPHYSICAL IMPLICATIONS FOR IN SILICO SIMULATION

* **1. Mandatory Capping of PSTi8 (`-CONH2`) and Explicit Ion/Electrostatic Treatment:**
  In molecular dynamics (MD) and docking pipelines, PSTi8 **must** be built with a C-terminal amide cap (`-CONH2`, net charge **$-5.0$** at pH 7.4, monoisotopic mass $2425.104\text{ Da}$, $\text{GRAVY} = -2.133$) rather than a default free carboxylate (`-COO-`, net charge $-6.0$). Because the `EEEEEMAV` penta-glutamate cluster creates an intense polyanionic surface potential while removing `VPQGLFRG` eliminates both the hydrophobic anchor (`Val-Pro-Gln-Gly-Leu-Phe`) and the C-terminal positive charge (`Arg28`), un-capped `-COO-` at Val21 introduces an artificial negative charge right at the C-terminal truncation boundary where PSTi8 engages GRP78.
* **2. Avoiding the `5E84` $L_{3,4}'$ (`TASDNQP` $\rightarrow$ `VGG`) Trap and the `5E85` Tethered-Peptide Trap:**
  Directly docking PST or PSTi8 into raw **`PDB 5E84`** will produce artifactual SBD poses because the native 7-residue substrate-binding loop $L_{3,4}$ (`457-TASDNQP-463`) is mutated to `VGG` and `Thr229` is mutated to `Ala229`. Similarly, docking into raw **`PDB 5E85`** without cleaving residues `638–653` (`SENLYFQGSNRLLLTG`) will fail because the SBD$\beta$ pocket is already occluded in *cis* by the covalently linked NR peptide. To model full-length wild-type human GRP78 accurately:
  * Use **`AF-P11021-F1`** (removing the `1–18` signal peptide) or homology-repair `5E84` by restoring `Ala229` $\rightarrow$ `Thr229` and grafting the wild-type $L_{3,4}$ loop (`457-TASDNQP-463`) from `5E85`.
  * For isolated NBD/ATPase docking (as performed by *Allu et al., Diabetes 2022* using `3LDO`), use **Chain B of `3LDL`** (which retains $\text{Mg}^{2+}$–ATP) or **`5EVZ`** (`1.85 Å`, $\text{Mg}^{2+}$–ADP–$\text{P}_i$), never metal-free Chain A of `3LDL`.
* **3. Ensemble Sampling Required for Intrinsically Disordered PST-29 and PSTi8 (`AF-P10645-F1`):**
  Because the entire Pancreastatin region (`268–319` in `P10645`) lies within a continuous intrinsically disordered region (`88–440`, $\text{pLDDT} < 50$ in `AF-P10645-F1`), static rigid-body docking of the raw AlphaFold coordinates is biophysically invalid. In accordance with *Allu et al. (2014, 2022)*, PST-29 and PSTi8 should undergo explicit-solvent replica-exchange or $\ge 200\text{ ns}$ MD conformational clustering prior to flexible/induced-fit docking to capture transient local $\alpha$-helicity and salt-bridge networks across the `PEGK...EEEEEMAV-NH2` backbone.
* **4. Mechanistic Decoupling of `Gly297Ser` (Agonist Gain-of-Function) vs. `Glu287Lys` (Inhibitor Overlap):**
  Because `Gly297Ser` (`rs9658664`) resides at position 25 inside the cleaved `VPQ[G]LFRG` tail, endogenous `PST-297S` in human carriers owes its gain-of-affinity for GRP78 and INSR to C-terminal structural stabilization that PSTi8 lacks. In contrast, `Glu287Lys` (`rs140089514`, position 15) falls squarely inside the `EEEEEMAV` motif of PSTi8, providing a direct in silico test case (mutating `Glu15` $\rightarrow$ `Lys15` in PSTi8) to quantify how disrupting the penta-glutamate electrostatic patch alters GRP78 binding free energy ($\Delta\Delta G_{\text{bind}}$).
