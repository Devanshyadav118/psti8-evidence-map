# Literature Search Strategy

**Document:** `docs/search_strategy.md`  
**Date of Initial Implementation:** 2026-10-31  
**Scope:** Systematic search and mapping of preclinical literature on PSTi8 (`PEGKGEQEHSQQKEEEEEMAV-NH₂`), the pancreastatin (PST)/chromogranin A (CHGA) axis, and related metabolic/sex-bias evidence (2018–2026).

---

## 1. Objectives

1. Identify all published primary studies evaluating the synthetic peptide PSTi8 in in vitro or in vivo disease and pharmacokinetic models.
2. Identify relevant preprints, bioanalytical method development papers, and reviews discussing PSTi8 mechanisms and therapeutic applications.
3. Map the broader biological context across six project threads:
   - **Thread 1:** Pancreastatin / CHGA biology and genetic variants (e.g., rs9658667 / Gly297Ser).
   - **Thread 2:** Signaling pathways (GRP78, IR/IRS, PKC, NOX3-JNK, AKT, AMPK).
   - **Thread 3:** PSTi8 preclinical evidence (pharmacodynamics, pharmacokinetics, and sex-specific reporting).
   - **Thread 4:** Disease context (T2D, NASH/MASH, PCOS, menopause, drug landscape).
   - **Thread 5:** Related/competing peptide approaches (e.g., catestatin, GLP-1/GIP agonists).
   - **Thread 6:** Preclinical methodology and reporting standards (ARRIVE guidelines, SAGER guidelines).

---

## 2. Databases & Information Sources

The search utilizes multiple international bibliographic databases, preprint servers, and open-access repositories:
- **PubMed / MEDLINE** (National Library of Medicine)
- **PubMed Central (PMC)** (Open-access full-text repository)
- **Europe PMC**
- **Google Scholar**
- **Crossref & DOI System** (`doi.org`)
- **Publisher Databases:** ScienceDirect (Elsevier), SpringerLink, MDPI, Frontiers Media, Oxford Academic
- **Preprint Servers & Registries:** bioRxiv, medRxiv, SSRN Electronic Journal, Research Square, ORCID record registries
- **Specialized Databases:** Pancreapedia, Semantic Scholar

---

## 3. Inclusion & Exclusion Criteria

### Inclusion Criteria
- **Molecular Identity:** Explicitly evaluates synthetic peptide PSTi8 (`PEGKGEQEHSQQKEEEEEMAV-NH₂`, PSTC$\Delta$8, CAS 2434805-63-5) or direct pancreastatin competitive antagonism.
- **Study Types:**
  - Primary preclinical in vivo animal studies (rodents, non-human mammals).
  - Primary in vitro cell culture studies (HepG2, 3T3-L1, L6, J774A.1, isolated islets/cells).
  - Bioanalytical assay validation and pharmacokinetic/ADME evaluations.
  - Preprints and conference proceedings with citable identifiers/abstracts.
  - Narrative and systematic reviews discussing pancreastatin inhibitor pharmacology or CHGA cleavage peptides in metabolic diseases.
- **Time Frame:** 2018 to present (2026), capturing the discovery of PSTi8 (`valicherla2018`) through current literature.
- **Language:** English.

### Exclusion Criteria
- Studies measuring chromogranin A (CHGA) or pancreastatin purely as a non-specific circulating biomarker for neuroendocrine tumors (NETs) without evaluation of pancreastatin receptor signaling or therapeutic inhibition.
- In vitro studies of unrelated peptides lacking sequence homology or antagonistic activity against pancreastatin.
- Non-peer-reviewed blog posts, unindexed forum comments, or materials lacking citable bibliometric metadata.

## 5. Verification & Extraction Workflow

To eliminate hallucination and unverified claims:
1. **Raw Staging:** All incoming search hits are staged raw in `data/staging/` before entry into registry or evidence tables.
2. **DOI & Bibliometric Verification:** Each DOI is opened and verified directly via official resolver endpoints (`doi.org`, PubMed API/web, Crossref metadata API). First authors, journal titles, volume/article numbers, and publication years are verified against primary publisher landing pages.
3. **Full-Text vs. Abstract Stratification:**
   - Open-access papers are downloaded, read in full, and documented with exact section locators.
   - Paywalled papers are labeled `abstract_only` (or `not_read`), and any extraction of animal sex or sample size is restricted strictly to what is stated verbatim in the abstract. Where sex is not stated in the abstract, it is labeled `NOT_READ` or `inferred_from_model` (for sex-specific surgical/endocrine models), never guessed.
4. **Discrepancy Logging:** Bibliometric errors (such as mismatched first authors or incorrect journal indexing in secondary databases) are logged in `docs/decisions/` and `docs/thread3_verification.md`.
