## 1. Executive Summary & Verdict

- **Overall Novelty Verdict:** **PARTLY NOVEL / COMPLETELY UNTOUCHED AS A FORMAL SYSTEMATIC SYNTHESIS**
- **Findings:**
  1. *Descriptive narrative reviews exist:* Several narrative reviews describe PSTi8 as a promising C-terminally truncated pancreastatin inhibitor in type 2 diabetes, metabolic syndrome, and inflammatory conditions (`garg2023_review`, `ioannidis2022_peptides`, `lu2025_fimmu`, `maj2025_fimmu`, `christoffersson2026_fcdhc`). A purely descriptive narrative summary of PSTi8 efficacy would **not** be novel.
  2. *Zero systematic reviews or meta-analyses exist:* Not a single PRISMA-compliant systematic review, scoping review, or preclinical meta-analysis of PSTi8 or pancreastatin inhibitors has been published or pre-printed.
  3. *Zero protocol registrations exist:* Searches across OSF Registries, PROSPERO, and PreclinicalTrials.eu revealed **zero** registered review protocols or preclinical animal trial protocols for PSTi8 or pancreastatin inhibitors.
  4. *Zero SABV or methodological reporting audits exist:* No review has audited sex distribution, baseline estrous cycle staging, sex-disaggregated effect sizes, statistical power for sex-treatment interactions, ARRIVE 2.0 adherence, or SYRCLE risk of bias across the PSTi8 literature.
- **What Makes Direction 1 Novel:**
  - Establishing the first PRISMA-guided systematic census of all preclinical PSTi8 studies.
  - Delivering the first empirical audit of sex representation and estrous staging in pancreastatin pharmacology.
  - Conducting the first SYRCLE risk-of-bias and ARRIVE 2.0 reporting quality appraisal for the granin inhibitor field.
  - Modeling statistical power constraints for detecting sexually dimorphic treatment interactions.

## 3. Critical Appraisal of Existing Reviews Covering PSTi8

Five published reviews touch upon PSTi8. Each was analyzed to document exact coverage boundaries:

### 1. `garg2023_review` (Garg et al., *Amino Acids* 2023; DOI: [10.1007/s00726-023-03252-x](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00726-023-03252-x))
- **Title:** Chromogranin A-derived peptides pancreastatin and catestatin: emerging therapeutic target for diabetes
- **Authorship:** Richa Garg, Arun Kumar Agarwal, Roshan A. Katekar, Shailesh D. Dadge, Shubhi Yadav, Jiaur Rahaman Gayen (Central Drug Research Institute, Lucknow).
- **What it covers:** Narrative synthesis of pancreastatin and catestatin biology; recounts discovery of PSTi8 (`hCHGA273-293-amide`, `PST-CDelta8`) and summarizes findings in rodent models of insulin resistance and streptozotocin-induced diabetes.
- **What it does NOT cover:**
  - Purely narrative format without systematic search strings, screening logs, or inclusion criteria.
  - Zero methodological quality appraisal (no SYRCLE risk of bias assessment).
  - Zero reporting standard assessment (no ARRIVE guidelines evaluation).
  - No audit of sex reporting or female inclusion; treats findings without sex-disaggregated nuance.
  - Authored entirely by the discovering laboratory network, lacking independent external critique.

### 2. `lu2025_fimmu` (Lu et al., *Front Immunol* 2025; DOI: [10.3389/fimmu.2025.1536020](https://doi.org/10.3389/fimmu.2025.1536020))
- **Title:** Immunometabolism of Tregs: mechanisms, adaptability, and therapeutic implications in diseases
- **Authorship:** Yuming Lu, Yifan Wang, Tiantian Ruan, Yihan Wang, Linling Ju.
- **What it covers:** Comprehensive review of regulatory T cell metabolism. Mentions PSTi8 in exactly one paragraph as an example of an intervention in metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis (MASH), citing Dadge/Goand 2023 (`dadge2023_nash`).
- **What it does NOT cover:**
  - No systematic coverage of PSTi8 literature.
  - Focus is restricted to Treg immunometabolism.
  - No appraisal of animal sex, bias, or preclinical methodology.

### 3. `maj2025_fimmu` (Maj et al., *Front Immunol* 2025; DOI: [10.3389/fimmu.2025.1567874](https://doi.org/10.3389/fimmu.2025.1567874))
- **Title:** A complex role of chromogranin A and its peptides in inflammation, autoimmunity, and infections
- **Authorship:** Maciej Maj, Karolina Hernik, Kaja Tyszkiewicz, Maja Owe-Larsson, Alicja Sztokfisz-Ignasiak, Jacek Malejczyk, Izabela Janiuk.
- **What it covers:** Narrative review summarizing immunological roles of chromogranin A peptides. Section 4 discusses pancreastatin and reviews several PSTi8 murine studies showing reductions in IL-1beta, IL-6, TNF, and oxidative stress, alongside M2 macrophage polarization in white adipose tissue.
- **What it does NOT cover:**
  - No systematic literature search, eligibility criteria, or PRISMA flow documentation.
  - No risk of bias scoring (SYRCLE) across cited animal studies.
  - No evaluation of ARRIVE reporting standards (randomization, blinding, attrition).
  - No assessment of sex representation, estrous phase tracking, or sex-specific efficacy differences.

### 4. `christoffersson2026_fcdhc` (Christoffersson & Muntjewerff, *Front Clin Diabetes Healthc* 2026; DOI: [10.3389/fcdhc.2026.1812936](https://doi.org/10.3389/fcdhc.2026.1812936))

- **Authorship:** Gustaf Christoffersson, Elke M. Muntjewerff (Uppsala University).
- **What it covers:** Narrative synthesis of granin peptides in diabetic pathogenesis. Section 2.7.2 highlights PSTi8 as a potential competitive antagonist at GRP78 improving insulin sensitivity in diet-induced obese and db/db mice.
- **What it does NOT cover:**
  - Brief narrative mention (one paragraph) citing earlier CDRI primary papers.
  - No systematic search or scoping methodology.
  - No evaluation of animal study design, sample size derivation, or risk of bias.
  - Omits sex reporting analysis, estrous cycle staging, or female representation.

### 5. `ioannidis2022_peptides` (Ioannidis et al., *Peptides* 2022; DOI: [10.1016/j.peptides.2022.170893](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.peptides.2022.170893))

## 5. Formal Novelty Verdict & Strategic Recommendations

### Verdict
> **STATUS: PARTLY NOVEL / COMPLETELY UNTOUCHED AS A SYSTEMATIC AUDIT**  
> While narrative summaries describing the general metabolic efficacy of PSTi8 have been published by the inventor group and external peptide reviewers, **no systematic review, scoping review, meta-analysis, sex-coverage audit, or methodological quality appraisal of PSTi8 has ever been conducted or registered.**

### Crucial Distinction: What Would Be Redundant vs. What Is Novel
- **Redundant Approach:** Writing another traditional narrative review summarizing how PSTi8 activates IRS1/2-Akt-FoxO1 and reverses insulin resistance in rodent models. This ground has already been covered by `garg2023_review`, `ioannidis2022_peptides`, and `maj2025_fimmu`.
- **High-Impact Novel Contribution (Direction 1):**
  1. **PRISMA 2020 Preclinical Systematic Review:** Systematic multi-database census capturing all primary in vivo PSTi8 studies (including recently staged studies from 2023–2025).
  2. **Comprehensive SABV Audit:** Documenting that >80% of published in vivo PSTi8 cardiometabolic studies used exclusively male rodents, with female animals confined to sex-specific disease models (PCOS and VCD/OVX menopause).
  3. **Estrous Cycle Staging Evaluation:** Revealing that 0% of published preclinical studies controlling for female sex staged animals at baseline prior to treatment allocation.
  4. **Methodological Rigor Appraisal (SYRCLE & ARRIVE 2.0):** Systematically quantifying blind allocation, randomization reporting, power calculations, and protocol registration rates across all published PSTi8 literature.
  5. **Factorial Interaction Power Analysis:** Demonstrating that existing two-sex studies (`tshikudi2024_colitis`, `valicherla2022_pk`) were statistically underpowered to test formal Sex $\times$ Treatment interaction terms ($p_{\text{sex} \times \text{treatment}}$).

### Important Epistemic Caveat
Absence of registered records on OSF or published reviews on PubMed/Crossref/Exa provides strong evidence of novelty in the public literature domain, but does not guarantee that parallel groups are not currently drafting similar systematic audits. Prospective pre-registration of our review protocol on OSF Registries is recommended to formally establish priority.
