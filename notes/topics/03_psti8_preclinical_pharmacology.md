# Topic 03: Preclinical Pharmacology, In Vivo Indications, and Efficacy Profiles of PSTi8

## 1. Executive Overview

PSTi8 (`hCHGA273-293-amide`, `PEGKGEQEHSQQKEEEEEMAV-NH2`) is a 21-amino-acid synthetic peptide designed as a competitive antagonist of pancreastatin. Over the past eight years (2018–2025), exactly **20 primary in vivo preclinical studies** (19 peer-reviewed papers and 1 conference abstract supplement) have evaluated its therapeutic potential across diverse rodent models of cardiometabolic and gastrointestinal disease.

This document synthesizes the pharmacological spectrum of PSTi8 across all 20 studies cataloged in [`data/evidence_table.csv`](file:///home/devansh/Downloads/Devansh%20projects/psti8-sex-bias-review/data/evidence_table.csv), mapping animal models, dosing regimens, routes, and physiological endpoints.

### 1. Type 2 Diabetes & Insulin Resistance (9 Studies)
- **Foundational Validation (`valicherla2018`):** First report of PSTi8 efficacy in three distinct male rodent models: high-fat diet (HFD) mice, genetic leptin-receptor deficient $db/db$ mice, and HFD + fructose + low-dose STZ rats. Daily intraperitoneal (i.p.) administration (2.5 mg/kg for 15 days) reduced fasting glycemia, improved oral glucose tolerance, increased skeletal muscle GLUT4 translocation, and suppressed hepatic glucose-6-phosphatase (G6Pase).
- **Combination Therapy (`singh2020_fetuina`):** Combined PSTi8 with metformin in male diabetic mice, showing additive suppression of circulating Fetuin-A and restoration of hepatic insulin sensitivity.
- **Skeletal & Renal Protection (`gupta2020_skeletal`, `garg2023_ejp`, `garg2023_aminoacids`, `gupta2019_dex`):** Demonstrated preservation of skeletal muscle Akt phosphorylation, mitigation of glucocorticoid (dexamethasone)-induced hepatic steatosis, and prevention of diabetic nephropathy progression in STZ rats.

### 2. Hepatic Steatosis & Non-Alcoholic Steatohepatitis (MASH) (3 Studies)
- **Mitochondrial & Oxidative Stress (`reza2021_mito`, `reza2022_lfs`):** Evaluated HFD-fed mice, showing that PSTi8 suppresses free fatty acid-induced reactive oxygen species (ROS) generation, blocks hepatic NOX3 induction, and prevents downstream JNK phosphorylation.
- **Dietary NASH Model (`dadge2023_nash`):** Investigated methionine-choline deficient (MCD) diet-fed mice, demonstrating that PSTi8 reduces hepatic lipid accumulation, blunts circulating ALT/AST elevation, suppresses pro-inflammatory cytokines (TNF, IL-1$\beta$, IL-6), and drives M2-like macrophage polarization in adipose tissue.

### 3. Female Endocrine & Reproductive Disorders (4 Studies)
- **Perimenopausal Metabolic Dysfunction (`singh2019_menopause`, `singh2020_heliyon`):** Modeled in female Sprague Dawley rats via chemical follicular depletion (4-vinylcyclohexene diepoxide, VCD) combined with bilateral ovariectomy and high-fat/high-fructose feeding. PSTi8 alleviated steatohepatitis, reduced hepatic ER stress (PERK, GRP78), modulated the SIRT-1/SREBP-1c lipogenic axis, and restored longitudinal estrous cyclicity.
- **Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) (`yadav2024_pcos_let`, `yadav2025_pcos_dhea`):** Evaluated letrozole-induced and DHEA-induced female rodent PCOS models. PSTi8 reduced hyperandrogenism, lowered circulating LH/FSH ratios, normalized ovarian steroidogenic enzyme expression (CYP17A1, CYP19A1), and mitigated accompanying systemic insulin resistance.

### 4. Inflammatory Bowel Disease & Colonic Homeostasis (2 Studies)
- **Dextran Sodium Sulfate (DSS) Colitis (`tshikudi2024_colitis`):** Evaluated intrarectal PSTi8 (2.5 mg/kg daily) in male and female C57BL/6 mice. Delayed colitis onset in males and protected colonic stem cells, whereas daily Disease Activity Index (DAI) was descriptively higher in females at 72–96 h (though cumulative DAI and macroscopic damage scores did not differ significantly).
- **Homeostatic Mucosal Maintenance (`tshikudi2024_jcag`):** In non-colitic mice, intrarectal PSTi8 induced sex-dependent gene expression shifts in colonic epithelial cells (reducing differentiation markers and increasing ATF6/CHOP selectively in females).

### 5. Pharmacokinetics & Bioanalysis (2 Studies)
- **Preclinical Pharmacokinetics (`valicherla2022_pk`):** Single-dose intravenous, intraperitoneal, and subcutaneous PK profiling in healthy Sprague Dawley rats ($n=3\text{--}6$ per sex per route). Bioavailability was highest via intraperitoneal administration (~68%), with a plasma elimination half-life ($t_{1/2}$) of approximately 30–45 minutes. No significant sex differences were observed in healthy animals.
- **Assay Development (`valicherla2020_jpba`):** Established LC-MS/MS bioanalytical detection in rodent plasma.

## 3. Laboratory Independence and Geographic Distribution

A critical finding of Phase 1 literature auditing is the structural concentration of the field:
1. **The CDRI Lucknow Network (18 of 20 studies, 90.0%):** Eighteen primary studies were conceived, synthesized, and published by the laboratory network of Jiaur Rahaman Gayen and colleagues at the CSIR-Central Drug Research Institute (CDRI), Lucknow, India.
2. **The Sherbrooke Gastroenterology Group (2 of 20 studies, 10.0%):** The only independent external replication of PSTi8 in the primary literature was conducted by the gastrointestinal research group of Jean-François Beaulieu and colleagues (Tshikudi et al.) at the Université de Sherbrooke, Canada, investigating colonic mucosal epithelial models.
3. **Translational Need:** While the CDRI network has demonstrated broad efficacy across numerous metabolic models, independent replication by unaffiliated laboratories in cardiometabolic disease (T2D, obesity, MASH) represents a vital prerequisite for clinical development [Inference].
