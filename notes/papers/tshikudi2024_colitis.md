# Tshikudi et al. 2024 — Colitis, Sex-Dependent CEC Profile

**Citation:** Tshikudi, Hutchison & Ghia, Int J Mol Sci, 2024  
**DOI:** [10.3390/ijms252312757](https://doi.org/10.3390/ijms252312757)  
**Access:** Full (MDPI open access)

## Study Design

- **Species/strain:** Mouse / C57BL/6 (Section 4.1, "Animals")
- **Sex:** Both male and female (Section 4.1)
- **n per group:** n=5–6 per sex per condition (Section 4.1; figure legends)
- **Disease model:** DSS-induced colitis (2% DSS, 5 days) + steady-state homeostasis (Section 4.2)
- **Route:** Intrarectal (Section 4.3)
- **Dose:** 10 µg per mouse, daily (Section 4.3)
- **Duration:** 7 days, Day 0–6 (Section 4.2–4.3; Fig 1A)

## Sex & Estrous

- **Estrous controlled:** NOT_READ — not mentioned in Methods
- **Sex analyzed as variable:** yes — all data presented separately by sex with within-sex comparisons (Sections 2.1–2.5; Figs 1–5)
- **Interaction tested:** NOT_READ — uses two-way ANOVA (Section 4.9) which can test interactions, but no explicit sex×treatment interaction term reported in results

## Statistics

- Two-way ANOVA + Tukey's post-hoc; Kruskal-Wallis + Dunn's for non-parametric (Section 4.9)

## Key Findings

- **Endpoints:** DAI, colon length, histological score, CEC markers (Lgr5, Muc2, ChgA, lysozyme), cytokines (TNF-α, IL-6, IL-1β, IFN-γ, IL-17A, IL-10, IL-22), Ki67, cleaved caspase-3 (Figs 1–5)
- **Result:** PSTi8 reduced colitis severity in males but worsened it in females, revealing sex-dependent effects on CEC maintenance via GPR78 (Abstract; Section 3)

## Notes

Only known PSTi8 study to include both sexes. Despite two-way ANOVA, the paper presents within-sex comparisons rather than explicitly reporting an interaction term. Estrous staging not mentioned. GPR78 used as receptor name.
