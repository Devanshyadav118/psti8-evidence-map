# Singh et al. 2020 — Fetuin-A Inhibition & Metformin Synergy

**Citation:** Singh et al., Heliyon, 2020  
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.heliyon.2020.e05133](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.heliyon.2020.e05133)  
**Access:** Full (Heliyon open access: PMC7533370)

## Study Design

- **Species/strain:** Mouse / C57BL/6 (Section 2.2, "Animals")
- **Sex:** Male only — "7–8 weeks old male C57BL/6 mice" (Section 2.2)
- **n per group:** n=6 per group (Section 2.3)
- **Disease model:** High-fat diet (12 weeks) plus single low-dose streptozotocin (100 mg/kg ip at week 4) induced type 2 diabetes (Section 2.3, Figure 1)
- **Route:** i.p. (Section 2.3)
- **Dose:** Acute PSTi8 5 mg/kg; chronic PSTi8 2 mg/kg alone, or 1 mg/kg in combination with metformin 150 mg/kg (Section 2.3)
- **Duration:** 10 consecutive days of therapeutic treatment following 12 weeks of dietary induction (Section 2.3)

## Sex & Estrous

- **Estrous controlled:** not_applicable (male animals exclusively)
- **Sex analyzed as variable:** no (single-sex cohort)
- **Interaction tested:** not_applicable

## Statistics

- Student's t-test, one-way or two-way ANOVA followed by Bonferroni post-hoc test; values expressed as mean ± SEM (Section 2.18)

## Key Findings

- **Endpoints:** Circulating and hepatic Fetuin-A mRNA/protein, IPGTT, IPITT, CLAMS metabolic energy expenditure, EchoMRI body composition, plasma lipid profile, liver glycogen, gluconeogenesis markers, and HepG2 assays (Sections 2.4–2.17; Figures 1–9)
- **Result:** Combination therapy of PSTi8 with low-dose metformin synergistically inhibited hepatic Fetuin-A expression, restored insulin sensitivity, improved lipid profiles, and enhanced energy expenditure in diabetic mice (Abstract; Sections 3.1–3.7; Section 5)

## Notes

Conducted exclusively in male C57BL/6 mice without female comparator or justification. Demonstrates synergistic anti-diabetic activity when PSTi8 is co-administered with low-dose metformin. CSIR-CDRI / Gayen laboratory.
