# Valicherla et al. 2018 — Discovery of PSTi8

**Citation:** Valicherla et al., Scientific Reports, 2018  
**DOI:** [10.1038/s41598-018-27018-8](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-018-27018-8)  
**Access:** Full (PMC open access, PMC6008391)

## Study Design

- **Species/strain:** Mouse (C57BL/6J, db/db BKS.Cg-Dock7m+/+Leprdb/J); Rat (Sprague Dawley for PK)
- **Sex:** Male only — "Male C57BL/6J mice (6–8 weeks)"; "Male db/db mice"; "Male Sprague Dawley rats" (Methods, "Animals" section)
- **n per group:** n=6–8 (Methods, "Animals"; figure legends)
- **Disease model:** (1) HFD-induced IR in C57BL/6J; (2) genetic T2D in db/db; (3) HFD+fructose+STZ T2D in C57BL/6J (Methods, "Animal models of diabetes mellitus")
- **Route:** i.p. (Methods, "Treatment protocol")
- **Dose:** 0.3, 1, 3 mg/kg (Methods, "Treatment protocol")
- **Duration:** 28 days (Methods, "Treatment protocol")

## Sex & Estrous

- **Estrous controlled:** not_applicable (males only)
- **Sex analyzed as variable:** no (one sex only)
- **Interaction tested:** not_applicable

## Statistics

- One-way ANOVA + Dunnett's post-hoc; unpaired t-test for two-group comparisons (Methods, "Statistical analysis")

## Key Findings

- **Endpoints:** Fasting glucose, OGTT, ITT, HbA1c, serum insulin, HOMA-IR, body weight, liver glycogen, hepatic glucose output, GLUT4 translocation (Figs 1–6)
- **Result:** PSTi8 at 1 mg/kg i.p. for 28 d restored glucose homeostasis across three T2D models comparably to metformin (Abstract; Figs 1–6)

## Notes

Foundational PSTi8 discovery paper. Males only; no justification for excluding females. Also includes in vitro (HepG2, L6 cell lines).
