# Singh et al. 2020 — Perimenopausal Steatohepatitis & ER Stress

**Citation:** Singh et al., Heliyon, 2020  
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.heliyon.2020.e05826](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.heliyon.2020.e05826)  
**Access:** Full (Heliyon open access, CC BY-NC-ND 4.0)

## Study Design

- **Species/strain:** Rat / Wistar (Section 2.2)
- **Sex:** Female only — "Female Wistar rats (8–10 weeks, 180–220 g)" (Section 2.2)
- **n per group:** n=6 (Section 2.2)
- **Disease model:** VCD-induced perimenopause + HFrD (20% fructose)-induced steatohepatitis/IR (Section 2.2)
- **Route:** i.p. (Section 2.2)
- **Dose:** PSTi8 1 mg/kg/day; metformin 100 mg/kg/day; alone and combined (Section 2.2)
- **Duration:** 90 d total; PSTi8 treatment 75 d (day 16–90, post-VCD) (Section 2.2)

## Sex & Estrous

- **Estrous controlled:** yes (monitored) — daily vaginal smear cytology to confirm VCD-induced perimenopause (Section 2.3)
- **Sex analyzed as variable:** no (female only)
- **Interaction tested:** not_applicable

## Statistics

- One-way ANOVA + Tukey's post-hoc (Section 2.9)

## Key Findings

- **Endpoints:** Body weight, estrous cyclicity, serum lipids (TC, TG, LDL, HDL), liver enzymes (SGOT, SGPT, ALP), fasting glucose, insulin, HOMA-IR, ER stress markers (GRP78, CHOP, ATF6, IRE1α, PERK), SIRT-1/SREBP-1c, liver histology (Figs 1–7)
- **Result:** PSTi8+metformin combination ameliorated perimenopause-induced steatohepatitis and ER stress by restoring SIRT-1 and suppressing SREBP-1c lipogenesis (Abstract; Section 4)

## Notes

Female-only by design (perimenopausal model). Estrous monitored as outcome, not synchronized before treatment. PSTi8 tested alone and combined with metformin. No male comparator. CDRI/Gayen lab.
