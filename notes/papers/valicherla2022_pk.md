# Valicherla et al. 2022 — Pharmacokinetics

**Citation:** Valicherla et al., Molecules, 2022  
**DOI:** [10.3390/molecules27020339](https://doi.org/10.3390/molecules27020339)  
**Access:** Full (MDPI open access)

## Study Design

- **Species/strain:** Rat / Sprague Dawley (Section 4.2, "Animals")
- **Sex:** Both male and female (Section 4.2)
- **n per group:** n=3 per sex per route (Section 4.5)
- **Disease model:** PK study in healthy rats (no disease model) (Section 4.5)
- **Route:** IV, IP, SC (Section 4.5)
- **Dose:** 1 mg/kg all routes (Section 4.5)
- **Duration:** Single dose; PK sampling over 8 h (Section 4.5)

## Sex & Estrous

- **Estrous controlled:** NOT_READ — no mention in Methods
- **Sex analyzed as variable:** yes — PK parameters reported separately by sex; paper states "no significant gender-based PK differences" (Section 2.5; Tables 4–5)
- **Interaction tested:** no — descriptive comparison between sexes; no formal sex×route interaction test reported (Section 2.5; Tables 4–5)

## Statistics

- Non-compartmental analysis (NCA) via Phoenix WinNonlin v8.3; descriptive stats (mean ± SD) (Section 4.6)

## Key Findings

- **Endpoints:** Plasma concentration–time profiles, Cmax, Tmax, AUC, t½, CL, Vd, F%, solubility, plasma stability, metabolic stability, protein binding (Sections 2.1–2.5; Tables 1–5; Figs 1–3)
- **Result:** PSTi8 has high solubility, stability, and bioavailability (~95% IP, ~78% SC) with no significant sex differences in PK (Abstract; Tables 4–5)

## Notes

Both sexes used but comparison is descriptive only — no inferential statistics on sex differences reported. Small n (n=3/sex/route). PK study, not efficacy. Includes in vitro (solubility, stability, protein binding) and in vivo components.
