## Key Findings

- **Endpoints:** Colonic epithelial proliferation (Ki67), differentiation markers (CK-20, goblet Muc2, tuft Dclk1), stem cells (Lgr5, Hopx, Ly6a), BMP4, lineage regulators (Atoh1, Hes1, Gfi1), and ER stress / apoptosis markers (ATF6, CHOP) (Abstract, "Methods")
- **Result:** PSTi8 reduced epithelial cell proliferation across both sexes, but selectively impaired differentiation, depleted mucus, and increased tuft cells and ER stress (CHOP) in females, indicating sex-dependent mucosal maintenance (Abstract, "Results" & "Conclusions")

## Notes

Evaluates basal homeostatic colon physiology in naive uninjured mice, contrasting with DSS colitis examined in Tshikudi et al., IJMS 2024. Demonstrates that both sexes were included and directly contrasted. Originates from the Jean-Eric Ghia laboratory (University of Manitoba).
